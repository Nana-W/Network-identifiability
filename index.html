Analysis

    The rank test holds with probability one at a random point; the degree is found by multistart Newton on h(θ) = h(θ*), so the reported d is a numerical count — a certificate needs the exact methods in the paper. Fiber points with any |coordinate| > 10⁶ are treated as spurious.
