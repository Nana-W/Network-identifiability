Analysis

    The rank test holds with probability one at a random point. For the degree: multistart Newton is a fast lower bound; the Bézout homotopy tracks the full multihomogeneous bound and reaches every isolated solution for a generic start; monodromy scales with d and adds the trace test; the generated script computes d exactly outside the browser. Fiber points with any |coordinate| > 10⁶ are treated as spurious.
